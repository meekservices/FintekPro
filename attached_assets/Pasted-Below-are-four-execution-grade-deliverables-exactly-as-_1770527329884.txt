How Your Earnings Are Calculated
FintekPro uses a performance-linked incentive model based on real financial transactions.
1. Direct Earnings (Agent Income)
When you sell a financial product, you receive a fixed percentage of the commission earned from that transaction.
This is your primary income.
2. Upline Incentives (Team Income)
If partners are working under you, you may receive incentives based on their successful transactions.
Incentives are calculated as a percentage of the remaining distributable amount
Earlier incentives reduce the base for deeper levels
This ensures fairness and long-term sustainability
3. No Income for Recruitment
You do not earn anything simply for adding partners.
Income is generated only when real financial products are sold to real clients.
4. Transparency
For every transaction, you can see:
Who sold it
Your role in the transaction
How your payout was calculated
Final credited amount
No hidden deductions. No manual overrides.
