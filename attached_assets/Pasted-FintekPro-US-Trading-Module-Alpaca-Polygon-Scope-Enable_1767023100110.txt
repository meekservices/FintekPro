FintekPro – US Trading Module (Alpaca + Polygon)
Scope
Enable Indian clients to:
View US stocks & ETFs (NASDAQ / NYSE, S&P 500 ETFs)
Receive AI/agent recommendations
Approve trades with consent
Execute trades via Alpaca
Track USD & INR performance
⚠️ Important
Alpaca supports US stocks & ETFs (there is no “US mutual fund” trading for retail APIs; US exposure is via ETFs like SPY, QQQ, VOO).
FintekPro acts as advisory + orchestration, not broker.

4. Compliance Rules (Hard Constraints)
Implement these checks before enabling trading UI:
Resident Individual (FEMA)
PAN available & verified
Risk profiling completed
LRS declaration accepted (USD 250k/year tracker)
Explicit client consent per trade
If any check fails → Disable Buy/Sell CTA
