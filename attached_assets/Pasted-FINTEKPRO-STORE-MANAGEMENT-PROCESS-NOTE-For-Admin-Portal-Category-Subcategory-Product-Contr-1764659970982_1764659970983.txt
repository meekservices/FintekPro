FINTEKPRO – STORE MANAGEMENT PROCESS NOTE
(For Admin Portal – Category / Subcategory / Product Control)
Version: 1.0
Prepared for: Fintekpro Admin System
URL: ...?admin=true
🎯 1. Objective
To provide Admin with full control over the product store, enabling:
Turn ON/OFF entire categories (e.g., Mutual Funds, Insurance, Loans, Bonds, Government Schemes).
Turn ON/OFF sub-categories (e.g., MF Direct, MF Regular, Term Insurance, Health Insurance, NCDs, Government Bonds).
Turn ON/OFF individual products—if required.
Display an Inquiry Page automatically when a product is OFF.
Control visibility for client, partner, agent, guest users.
Maintain audit logs per regulatory requirement.

🧭 5. UX + UI Flow for Admin
Step 1: Admin opens the Store Panel
URL automatically loads:
.../?admin=true#store
Step 2: Admin sees Category-Level Controls
Example UI:
[Mutual Funds]            [Toggle ON/OFF]
[Bonds & Debentures]      [Toggle ON/OFF]
[Insurance]               [Toggle ON/OFF]
[Loans]                   [Toggle ON/OFF]
[Unlisted Shares]         [Toggle ON/OFF]
[Govt Schemes]            [Toggle ON/OFF]
Step 3: Clicking any category opens Subcategories
Mutual Funds
  ✔ Direct Plans        [Toggle]
  ✔ Regular Plans       [Toggle]
Step 4: Clicking a Subcategory shows Product List
Direct Plans (active)
   HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund - Direct Plan             [ON/OFF]
   ICICI Bluechip Direct Plan                         [ON/OFF]
   SBI Smallcap Direct Plan                            [ON/OFF]
Step 5: When Admin turns OFF
A) Category
→ All subcategories and products automatically OFF
B) Subcategory
→ All products under it automatically OFF
C) Product
→ Only the specific product is hidden
🛑 6. Client-Side Behavior with Toggles
Scenario 1: Category OFF
Clients see an Inquiry Page:
This product category is temporarily unavailable.
Would you like a callback?
[Request Callback]
Scenario 2: Subcategory OFF
Show:
This sub-section is being upgraded.
Please try again later.
Scenario 3: Product OFF
Show:
This product is not available right now.
Please contact support.
🔐 7. Access Control
Role	Permission
Superadmin	Full control (Add/Delete/Toggle)
Admin	Toggle ON/OFF only
Business Development Head	Toggle subcategories & products
Master Agent	Cannot control store
Partner	Cannot control store
Agent	Cannot control store
Client	Only sees available items
🧾 8. Mandatory Audit Logging
Whenever admin toggles something:
Example log entry:
Admin: skm@fintekpro.com
Action: TOGGLE
Target: Subcategory
Target ID: 22
Before: is_enabled = true
After: is_enabled = false
Timestamp: 2025-12-05 10:22:44
Stored in: store_audit_logs
Regulatory requirement: store for 7 years.
