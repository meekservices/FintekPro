Below is a bank-wise payload mapping and a revenue & commission flow design for Option A (DSA / Sub-DSA), written to be implementation-ready for Fintekpro.
PART 1 — Bank-Wise Payload Mapping
(ICICI / HDFC / Axis / Kotak)
Principle: One internal canonical payload → bank-specific adapters
This keeps Fintekpro independent of bank changes.

PART 2 — Revenue & Commission Flow Design
A. Revenue Streams for Fintekpro
Revenue Type	Source
DSA Commission	Bank payout
Platform Fee	Client / partner
Priority Routing	Lender placement
Analytics (later)	Banks / NBFCs
B. Typical Commission Structure (India – Indicative)
Product	Commission
Personal Loan	0.5% – 2.0%
Business Loan	1.0% – 3.0%
LAP	0.3% – 1.0%
Example:
₹5,00,000 personal loan × 1% = ₹5,000 commission
C. Commission Flow (Sub-DSA Model)
Bank
 ↓ (payout)
Master DSA
 ↓ (revenue share)
Fintekpro
Typical split:
Bank → Master DSA: 1.5%
Master DSA → Fintekpro: 60–80% of that
D. Internal Commission Ledger (Recommended)
Table: commission_ledger
Field	Purpose
loan_id	Traceability
bank	Source
sanctioned_amount	Base
commission_rate	%
gross_commission	Bank payout
fintekpro_share	Net revenue
status	pending / paid
E. Optional Client-Side Pricing (Allowed)
Model	Notes
Zero-fee	Higher conversion
Success fee	Disclosed upfront
Subscription	SME / partner channel
⚠ RBI requires full disclosure — no hidden fees.
F. Break-Even Illustration
100 loans/month
Avg ticket: ₹4L
Avg commission: 1%
→ Monthly gross = ₹4,00,000
→ Even at 60% share = ₹2,40,000
No LOS license. Pure margin.
