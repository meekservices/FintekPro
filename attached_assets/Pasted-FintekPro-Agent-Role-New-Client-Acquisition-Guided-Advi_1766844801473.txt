FintekPro – Agent Role: New Client Acquisition & Guided Advisory
OBJECTIVE
Enable an Agent to acquire new clients by:
Analyzing the client’s existing portfolio (PAN-level / uploaded)
Using AI-generated insights to identify gaps and opportunities
Creating AI-assisted or fully agent-generated investment proposals
Providing structured client hand-holding from onboarding → proposal → approval → execution
Converting external portfolios into FintekPro-managed relationships over time
CORE PRINCIPLES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
Client acquisition is advisory-first, not transaction-first
AI assists; Agent owns the conversation
Client consent is required at every actionable step
Suitability, risk profiling, and disclosures are mandatory
All actions are auditable and admin-approved where required
