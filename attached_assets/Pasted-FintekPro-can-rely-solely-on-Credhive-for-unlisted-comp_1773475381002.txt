FintekPro can rely solely on Credhive for unlisted company intelligence, but the system architecture should be designed carefully to avoid data gaps and vendor lock-in.
Since you are building FintekPro as an institutional research platform, the correct approach is to treat Credhive as the primary data provider and build your own analytics layer on top of it.
1. Recommended architecture with Credhive as the only source
Credhive API
      ↓
FintekPro Data Ingestion Layer
      ↓
Company Intelligence Database
      ↓
Analytics & Valuation Engine
      ↓
Research Note Generator
      ↓
Client / Advisor Dashboard
Credhive becomes the raw intelligence layer, while FintekPro creates the value-added research layer.

6. Key limitation to understand
Credhive typically provides summarized financial data, not:
full MCA XBRL
full annual report PDFs
full shareholding tables in some cases
Therefore FintekPro should focus on analytics rather than document archiving.
7. Best strategy for FintekPro
Treat Credhive like Bloomberg data feed.
Credhive → Data
FintekPro → Intelligence
Your differentiation becomes:
valuation models
research reports
screening tools
risk scoring
portfolio analytics
✅ Conclusion
Using Credhive as the sole unlisted company data provider is feasible if FintekPro builds:
its own financial analytics engine
its own valuation models
its own research automation layer
This keeps data cost low while still producing institutional-grade analysis.
