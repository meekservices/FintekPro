NASDAQ & S&P 500 Investing for Indian Residents (LRS-Compliant)
1. Purpose
The US-Invest module enables Indian resident clients to discover, analyse, receive AI/agent recommendations, approve, and execute US equity & ETF investments in a fully RBI-LRS, FEMA, and US broker-compliant manner.
FintekPro acts as:
Advisory & orchestration platform
Compliance gatekeeper
Portfolio & reporting engine
FintekPro does NOT:
Hold custody
Act as broker
Directly connect to exchanges
Execution & custody are handled by a licensed US broker (recommended: DriveWealth).

6. Compliance Flow (Non-Negotiable)
Step 1: India Eligibility Check
Resident Individual (FEMA)
PAN validated
LRS balance available (USD 250k/year)
SEBI-aligned risk profile completed
❌ If any check fails → US-Invest disabled
Step 2: US Broker Account Opening
Handled via broker-hosted flow:
Passport
PAN
Address proof
W-8BEN
FATCA
FintekPro stores reference IDs only.
Step 3: LRS Declaration
Purpose code: Overseas investment
Digital declaration
Stored for audit (7 years)
Step 4: Trade Approval
AI / Agent recommendation
Client approval (OTP + consent)
Compliance validation
Order placement via broker API
