4. AI Recommendation Logic (Implementation Point #4)
4.1 Portfolio Diagnostics Engine

AI computes:

Asset allocation deviation

Risk score mismatch

Concentration (single stock >10%, single AMC >25%)

MF overlap >30%

Duration mismatch in debt

Illiquidity risk

4.2 Risk Scoring Formula (Illustrative)
Portfolio Risk Score =
Σ (Asset Weight × Asset Risk Rating)

Risk Rating Scale:
Equity Large Cap: 6
Equity Mid/Small: 8–9
Debt AAA: 2
MLD: 7
AIF Cat III: 9

4.3 Recommendation Trigger Rules
Condition	Action
Risk score > client tolerance	Reduce equity
MF overlap > 30%	Switch
Cash > 10%	Deploy
Single stock > 15%	Partial sell
Debt duration mismatch	Replace

Expected portfolio improvement
