Below is a practical, credit-desk–usable mapping of Indian Banks & NBFCs for Builder / Project Funding, aligned to how FintekPro’s lender-appetite engine should actually behave in production.

🏦 1️⃣ PSU BANKS (Large Ticket, Conservative Credit)
Typical Use
Late-stage projects
Low-risk cities
Strong developer balance sheet
Lower pricing, slower turnaround
Lender	Ticket Size	Preferred Products	Key Credit Filters
State Bank of India	₹50–500 Cr	Construction Finance, LRD	DSCR ≥ 1.3, RERA compliant, escrow
Bank of Baroda	₹40–300 Cr	Project Funding	Strong presales, clear title
Punjab National Bank	₹30–200 Cr	Construction Finance	PSU-led syndication
Canara Bank	₹25–150 Cr	Builder Funding	Tier-1 only
System Rule
Only show PSU banks if project_stage >= Under Construction AND city in Tier-1
🏦 2️⃣ PRIVATE SECTOR BANKS (Selective, Faster)
Typical Use
Brand developers
Metro & Tier-1 cities
Structured deals (escrow + monitoring)
Lender	Ticket Size	Preferred Products	Key Credit Filters
HDFC Bank	₹50–400 Cr	Construction Finance, LRD	Top-tier developers only
ICICI Bank	₹40–300 Cr	Project Funding	Presales velocity strong
Axis Bank	₹30–250 Cr	LRD, Inventory Finance	Escrow + sales traction
Kotak Mahindra Bank	₹50–350 Cr	Structured Builder Funding	Complex structures allowed
System Rule
Require developer_track_record >= 2 completed projects
🏢 3️⃣ HOUSING FINANCE COMPANIES (HFCs)
Typical Use
Mid-stage construction
Residential focus
Faster approvals than banks
Lender	Ticket Size	Preferred Products	Key Credit Filters
HDFC Ltd	₹30–300 Cr	Construction Finance	Residential only
LIC Housing Finance	₹25–200 Cr	Builder Funding	Strong promoter net worth
PNB Housing Finance	₹20–150 Cr	Project Funding	RERA mandatory
Indiabulls Housing Finance	₹30–250 Cr	Structured / Bridge	Higher yield tolerance
🏗️ 4️⃣ NBFCs – REAL ESTATE SPECIALISTS (High Yield)
Typical Use
Early-stage projects
Land purchase
Weak presales but strong location
Higher pricing (14–22%)
Lender	Ticket Size	Preferred Products	Key Credit Filters
L&T Finance	₹20–200 Cr	Construction, Land	Engineering-led diligence
Tata Capital	₹25–250 Cr	Builder / Bridge	Flexible structuring
Aditya Birla Finance	₹30–300 Cr	Mezzanine, Bridge	IRR focused
Piramal Capital	₹50–500 Cr	Mezzanine, Special Situations	Turnaround deals
System Rule
Allow NBFCs if project_stage == Land OR Early Construction
