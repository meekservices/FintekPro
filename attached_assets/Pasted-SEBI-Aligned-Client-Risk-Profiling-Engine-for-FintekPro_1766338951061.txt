SEBI-Aligned Client Risk Profiling Engine for FintekPro
1. Objective
Implement a regulatory-compliant, auditable, and AI-enhanced client risk profiling framework that:
Classifies clients into 5 standardized risk profiles
Uses a SEBI-aligned risk questionnaire
Maps risk profiles to product eligibility & suitability
Supports dynamic re-scoring based on financial behavior and life events
Acts as a hard eligibility gate for all advisory and transaction flows
2. Risk Profile Taxonomy (System Master)
Create a master table risk_profiles_master.
Code	Profile Name	Risk Band	Description
RP1	Conservative	Very Low	Capital protection priority
RP2	Moderately Conservative	Low–Moderate	Income with limited volatility
RP3	Moderate / Balanced	Moderate	Growth + stability
RP4	Moderately Aggressive	Moderate–High	Long-term wealth creation
RP5	Aggressive	High	Maximum return with volatility
System Rules
Risk profile stored at PAN level
Goal-specific override allowed (sub-profile per goal)
One active primary profile at any time
3. SEBI-Aligned Risk Profiling Questionnaire
Create a configurable module risk_questionnaire.
3.1 Questionnaire Categories & Weightage
Category	Weight

Net Worth & Surplus	20%
Investment Horizon	20%
Risk Tolerance (Behavioral)	15%
Market Experience	10%
Total	100%
3.2 Sample Questions (Mandatory)
A. Age
< 30 → Score 5
30–45 → Score 4
45–60 → Score 2
60 → Score 1
B. Income Stability
Fixed salary + emergency fund → 5
Variable income → 3
Unstable / dependent → 1
C. Investment Horizon
10 years → 5
5–10 years → 4
3–5 years → 3
< 3 years → 1
D. Risk Tolerance (Scenario-Based)
“Your portfolio falls 20% in 6 months. You would:”
Invest more → 5
Hold → 3

E. Market Experience
10 years → 5
5–10 years → 4
< 3 years → 2
None → 1
3.3 Technical Notes
All questions configurable from Admin Panel
Each answer mapped to numeric score
Questionnaire versioning mandatory (audit trail)
4. Risk Scoring Engine (Rules-Based Core)
Create service: riskScoringService.
4.1 Score Calculation
Final Risk Score = Σ (Answer Score × Category Weight)
4.2 Score to Profile Mapping
Score Range	Risk Profile
0 – 30	Conservative
31 – 45	Moderately Conservative
46 – 60	Moderate
61 – 75	Moderately Aggressive
76 – 100	Aggressive
4.3 Mandatory SEBI Overrides
Force downgrade risk profile if:
Age > 60 AND horizon < 3 years
No emergency fund AND aggressive answers
High liabilities vs income ratio
Log override reason explicitly.

6. AI + Rules Hybrid Dynamic Risk Engine
Create service: dynamicRiskEngine.
6.1 Trigger Events for Re-Scoring
Large inflow / outflow
New liabilities added
Income change
Age band crossing
Market behavior (panic selling / over-trading)
6.2 AI Inputs

Drawdown reaction history
Asset concentration

6.3 AI Outputs

Explanation text (mandatory for audit)
6.4 Governance Rules
AI can recommend, not enforce
Final risk profile requires:
Client reconfirmation OR
Agent + Client approval
