# FintekPro Risk Governance & Quantitative Risk Policy (RGP v1.0)

**Standard Version**: RGP-1.0.0  
**Effective Date**: October 2026  
**Governing Authority**: FintekPro Investment & Risk Committee (IRC)  
**Regulatory Framework**: SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 (Regulation 17 — Suitability), SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, and FASP-AI v3.0 Quantitative Risk Architecture

---

## 1. Executive Mandate & Purpose

FintekPro provides institutional-grade portfolio advisory and wealth management infrastructure for SEBI-registered Investment Advisors (RIAs), Chartered Accountants (CAs), and institutional clients. 

The **FintekPro Risk Policy** establishes hard, deterministic risk guardrails across:
1. **Client Suitability**: Ensuring no investor is exposed to financial instruments exceeding their assessed risk capacity.
2. **Portfolio Risk Budgets**: Mathematically limiting volatility, market beta, drawdown, and concentration.
3. **AI Advisory Guardrails (FASP-AI)**: Preventing algorithmic hallucination, return promises, and unapproved execution.
4. **Execution & Demat Safety**: Safeguarding live market orders against slippage, illiquidity, and fractional share leakage.

## 2. Client Risk Profiling & Suitability Gate (SEBI Reg 17)

### 2.1 The 5-Tier SEBI Risk Scale
Every retail and institutional client must complete a psychometric and financial capacity assessment mapping them into one of five standardized risk levels:

| Tier | Risk Category | Score Range (1–100) | Maximum Permitted Equity | Maximum Volatility ($\sigma$) | Permitted Asset Classes |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Conservative** | 0 – 25 | 15% | 8% p.a. | Liquid Funds, T-Bills, AAA Sovereign/Corporate Bonds, Arbitrage Funds |
| **2** | **Moderate** | 26 – 50 | 45% | 14% p.a. | Conservative Hybrids, Large-Cap Bluechips, Target Maturity Debt, Gold ETFs |
| **3** | **Moderately High** | 51 – 70 | 70% | 20% p.a. | Flexi-Cap Equities, Mid-Cap Leaders, Multi-Asset Funds, Base Metal Benchmarks |
| **4** | **Aggressive** | 71 – 85 | 90% | 26% p.a. | Mid & Small-Cap Equities, Sectoral/Thematic Funds, International Equities |
| **5** | **Very Aggressive / HNI** | 86 – 100 | 100% | 32% p.a. | High-Beta Growth Stocks, Special Situations, Unlisted Equities, Private Credit |

### 2.2 Hard Suitability Gates (`requireSuitability`)
- **Block Rule**: If a proposed model portfolio or recommendation has a higher risk tier than the client's current profile, the platform **blocks order placement automatically** with an HTTP 403 `SUITABILITY_BREACH`.
- **Annual Recertification**: Under SEBI Regulation 17(c), client risk profiles expire exactly **365 days** from assessment. Any profile older than 365 days triggers a `STALE_RISK_PROFILE` gate that blocks rebalancing and new investments until re-assessment is logged.

## 3. Quantitative Portfolio Risk Budgets (Model Portfolios)

All model portfolios generated by the Black-Litterman Engine, Mean-Variance Optimizer, or Quantitative Research Desk must pass the [`PortfolioRiskGuard`](file:///Users/sangrammohanty/Documents/GitHub/FintekPro/server/services/portfolio-risk-guard.ts) before publication or auto-rebalance.

### Risk Budgets by Portfolio Mandate:
```typescript
interface RiskBudget {
  maxVolatility: number;    // % annualised standard deviation
  maxDrawdown: number;      // % maximum peak-to-trough drop
  betaCeiling: number;      // portfolio weighted beta vs. Nifty 50
  sharpeFloor: number;      // minimum hurdle Sharpe ratio (Rf = 6.5%)
  maxSingleWeight: number;  // % max weight in any single asset
  maxSectorWeight: number;  // % max combined weight in any single sector
}
```

| Portfolio Mandate | Max Volatility ($\sigma$) | Max Drawdown | Beta Ceiling ($\beta$) | Sharpe Floor | Max Single Holding | Max Sector Weight |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Conservative Capital Preserver** | 8.0% | -6.0% | 0.40 | 1.20 | 30.0% | 50.0% |
| **Moderate Balanced Growth** | 16.0% | -14.0% | 1.00 | 0.70 | 25.0% | 40.0% |
| **All-Weather Multi-Asset** | 12.0% | -10.0% | 0.70 | 0.90 | 25.0% | 40.0% |
| **Aggressive Wealth Builder** | 26.0% | -24.0% | 1.60 | 0.40 | 15.0% | 35.0% |
| **High Alpha Opportunities** | 30.0% | -28.0% | 1.90 | 0.30 | 12.0% | 30.0% |

### Hard vs. Soft Breach Policy:
- **Hard Breach**: Violating `maxSingleWeight`, `betaCeiling`, or `maxDrawdown` **blocks auto-apply** completely. Human RIA review and signed override are required.
- **Soft Breach**: Minor volatility or Sharpe ratio warnings allow execution but emit an alert log: `{ event: "RISK_SOFT_WARNING", portfolio_id, metrics }`.

## 4. Concentration, Liquidity & Instrument Limits

To prevent catastrophic single-stock contagion and market liquidity entrapment:

1. **Single Stock Concentration Cap**:
   - Maximum weight for any single equity in a diversified portfolio is capped at **15%** (large-caps) and **7%** (mid/small-caps).
2. **Sector Concentration Cap**:
   - Maximum exposure to any single sector (e.g. Financials, IT, Auto) cannot exceed **35%** of portfolio net asset value.
3. **Liquidity Guardrail**:
   - Any equity position size must be $\le 10\%$ of the 30-day Average Daily Volume (ADV) on NSE/BSE to guarantee execution without adverse price impact.
4. **Credit Quality Minimums**:
   - Direct corporate bond investments require minimum credit rating of **AA** from a SEBI-registered rating agency (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE). Instruments rated A+ or below are restricted to accredited HNI mandates.

## 5. AI Advisory Risk Framework (FASP-AI v1.0)

Under the FintekPro AI Governance architecture:

1. **Decision Support Only (Human-In-The-Loop)**:
   - AI algorithms are strictly decision-support systems. AI **never autonomously transmits orders** to exchanges without client confirmation or RIA/CA authorization.
2. **Prohibition of Return Guarantees**:
   - The AI advisory engine is strictly prohibited from generating deterministic return claims (e.g. *"Will yield 15%"*, *"Guaranteed profit"*, *"Zero risk"*).
   - Every recommendation must incorporate risk factors, probability cones, and downside risk ranges.
3. **Confidence Scoring & Graceful Downgrade**:
   - Every algorithmic recommendation emits a `confidence_score` (0.00 to 1.00).
   - If confidence is **$< 0.85$**, the engine automatically downgrades the recommendation intensity and routes the advisory dossier to a human advisor for review.
4. **Mandatory Audit Logging**:
   - All AI advisory outputs are logged immutably:
     ```json
     {
       "event": "AI_ADVICE_GENERATED",
       "user_id": "<uuid>",
       "risk_profile": "<tier>",
       "factors_considered": ["PE", "ROE", "DebtToEquity", "Drawdown"],
       "model_version": "FASP-AI v3.0",
       "timestamp": "<iso_timestamp>"
     }
     ```

## 6. Execution & Demat Order Risk Controls

When translating portfolio weights into live Demat orders via Upstox:

1. **Whole-Share Quantization**:
   - Indian exchanges (NSE, BSE, MCX) do not permit fractional shares. All order quantities are strictly rounded down to whole integers:
     $$\text{Quantity} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Target Allocation (INR)}}{\text{Live LTP (INR)}} \right\rfloor$$
2. **Cash Drag & Slippage Buffer**:
   - An execution buffer of **$1.0\%$ to $2.0\%$** unallocated cash is retained in every rebalance to prevent account margin shortfall due to market volatility during order transit.
3. **Price Protection Band**:
   - Market orders are paired with a maximum allowable slippage limit of **$0.50\%$** over Last Traded Price (LTP). If execution exceeds this threshold, orders are rejected to protect client capital.
